# Glow Lights Card

🇬🇧 English · 🇳🇱 [Nederlands](lights.nl.md)

![Lights](../images/lights.png)

Lights for phones: one row per room. Tap the orb to switch the room, drag the bar for brightness, tap the name to expand its lights. **All off** at the top.

Options: `title` (or `false`), `rooms` (`entity`, `name`, `icon`, `color`, `lights`), `open`, `glow`.
